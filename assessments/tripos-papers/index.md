# Tripos paper index (pointers only)

The papers are **University of Cambridge copyright**. The page that hosts them states: "All papers are
copyright by the University of Cambridge and may not be reproduced without permission." No paper is
stored in this repository, and `.gitignore` blocks PDFs here. This file records where each paper is
and how the questions map to the syllabus register.

Source page: https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/pastpapers/maths-ia/2025 (retrieved 21/10/2026).
All 2025 questions in one file: https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/pastpapers/files/2025/list_ia.pdf
(not examined for this baseline).

## Edition note (decided)

These papers are the **2025** Part IA papers (academic year 2024-25). The Schedules and Guide used for the syllabus register are the **2026-27**
editions, and the syllabus may have changed between the two. The curriculum director has **accepted the 2025 papers for examination practice, with the
edition differences acknowledged** ([director decision D-03](../../governance/director-decisions.md)). A question that touches material absent from the
2026-27 Schedules should be treated as out of syllabus, and the assessor should say so when setting it.

Each paper has Section I (questions 1 to 4, shorter) and Section II (questions 5 to 12, each worth twice a
Section I question). Each section is split equally between two courses. Candidates may attempt all
Section I questions and at most five Section II questions, no more than three on the same course
(Schedules 2026-27, printed p.7).

## Use

Past papers are for **assessment and independent problem solving**, not first teaching. The assessor
selects questions and records, in the evidence file, the paper, question label, the item ids it
exercised and the hints given. Solutions are not provided on the Faculty page; do not store or invent
model solutions here.
