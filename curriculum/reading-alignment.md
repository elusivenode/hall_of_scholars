# Reading alignment: syllabus items against verified contents

Status: **evidence for the curriculum director's choice of reading resources.** For each course, each syllabus item is compared with the **verified** contents of the proposed resources (see [bibliography.md](../references/bibliography.md) for what was and was not seen).

**Codes.** `C` covered: the topic is named in a verified contents list or in body text that was read. `P` partial: a related topic is named but not this item's specifics, or only one part of the item. `N` not seen: verified contents or text do not show it. `U` unknown: no usable contents exist for that resource, so nothing can be said. **A code is about whether a topic is named, never about depth, rigour or exercise supply.** A resource's absence from a column (for example Burn for Numbers and Sets) means it was verified only at chapter level and is described in the bibliography instead.

Item labels are the librarian's own short descriptions; the full summaries are in [syllabus.md](syllabus.md).
