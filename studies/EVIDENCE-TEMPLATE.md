# Evidence record - TEMPLATE

Copy to `studies/<course>/work/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` (or to `assessments/diagnostic/` for the diagnostic) and fill every section. The validator
requires the four sections below to be non-empty. Never reconstruct evidence from memory: if the work was not captured, no evidence exists.

Cite the item ids and the source of the problem at the top. Do not paste textbook or Tripos paper text (copyright); give a reference.

**Items:** IA-XX-NNN
**Source of the problem:** resource and section, or Tripos paper and question label
**Date:** DD/MM/YYYY

## Problem

The problem as set, or a precise reference to it.

## Student work

The student's actual written work, transcribed or scanned (give the path to a scan). Unedited.

## Hints given

Each hint with its level (`none`, `minor`, `substantial`), or "none", or "unknown" if the history is genuinely unavailable.

## Assessor evaluation

The assessor's evaluation of the argument, including errors found and gaps left open. An Assessment Decision, if any, is recorded separately in
`progress/mastery.yaml` (see `governance/assessment-standard.md` section 7).
