# Bibliography

A selective bibliography: one proposed primary text per course, a few supplements, the free course-matched notes, and the Faculty example sheets. Cambridge's full "Appropriate books" lists are in the Schedules (see [source-register.md](source-register.md)); books on those lists that are not here have **not** been evaluated and are not proposed.

**Verification status**

- `verified` - the facts relied on (identity, edition, and the contents used) were seen in a primary source (publisher page, author page, library record, or the document itself) on 21/10/2026.
- `partially verified` - part of that was seen; the entry says what was and was not.
- `unverified` - nothing was confirmed from a primary source. The entry gives Cambridge's own citation only, and **no chapter pointers are given anywhere for it**.

No chapter, section or exercise identifier appears in this repository unless it was read from an actual contents list or document. Where a book's contents were not seen, the reading map says so.

Everything was retrieved on 21/10/2026. ISBNs are omitted wherever they were not seen on a primary page.
