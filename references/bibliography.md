# Bibliography

A selective bibliography: for each course a working primary resource, a few companions, the free course-matched notes, and the Faculty example sheets. Cambridge's full "Appropriate books" lists are in the Schedules (see [source-register.md](source-register.md)); books on those lists that are not here have **not** been evaluated and are not proposed.

Every entry carries **four separate statuses**, never merged into one:

- **Bibliographic** - author, title, edition, publisher and year confirmed on a primary page (publisher, author, library catalogue or the document itself): `verified`, `partial` or `unverified`.
- **Contents** - a real contents list (chapter and section titles) was seen on a primary page: `verified`, `partial` (for example chapter titles only) or `unverified`. A search-result summary or a tool paraphrase does not count as seen.
- **Alignment** - the verified contents were compared with the syllabus items: `checked` (an item-by-item comparison was completed, whatever its result, including items marked not seen), `partial` (incomplete, or only chapter-level contents), `not-checked`. The comparison is recorded in [curriculum/reading-alignment.md](../curriculum/reading-alignment.md). Alignment means a topic is **named** in verified contents; it says nothing about depth, rigour or the supply of exercises.
- **Availability** - to the student: free online (with licence), owned, purchase, library, secondhand, or unknown, with what was and was not checked.

No chapter, section or exercise identifier appears in this repository unless it was read from an actual contents list or document. Everything was retrieved on 21/10/2026. ISBNs are omitted wherever they were not seen on a primary page. Outstanding verification tasks are in [verification-tasks.md](verification-tasks.md).
